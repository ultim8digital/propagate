Community rules

Choose a city

Choose a city manually or allow a one-time location lookup. The app saves the city and approximate coordinates, without background location tracking. Do not put your precise address or private contact details in public listings.

Online services

In person or online. Add a result check and a future deadline for each service

Sign in to see your listings

Browsing does not require sign-in. Posting and exchanging require iCloud, a profile and acceptance of the rules. Apple provides an app-specific account identifier; the app does not receive your Apple ID password or email address.

Cancel proposal or report a problem

If either part has been handed over or completed, the exchange is marked as a problem and the lots stay reserved while it is resolved.

Report listing

Use Report and Block when something is wrong. We review every report within 24 hours; content that breaks these rules is removed and its author banned.
