FAQ

Good to know

Answers before you get started.

Can I buy or sell plants?

No. Propagate is for swaps. Each offer shows what both growers give; no payments are handled in the app.

What can I swap?

Plants, cuttings, seeds, divisions and gardening supplies such as pots and soil. Read the community rules before posting.

Is my address visible?

No. Your profile shows your city. If you choose to share your location, it is rounded to about one kilometre before saving. Arrange meet-ups in a public place.

Why are there few listings near me?

Propagate is a young community. Availability depends on your city. Post your first offering or wanted plant and invite a fellow grower to join.
